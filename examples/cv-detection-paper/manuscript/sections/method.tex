\section{Method}
\label{sec:method}

Method X is an anchor-free detection head with three branches per location:
classification, regression, and centerness. The branches share a four-conv
tower per task that is reused across all FPN levels. This is the
\emph{shared per-level tower} of~\citet{chen2023anchorfreebaseline} and is
implemented by the \texttt{AnchorFreeHead} class
(\texttt{src/models/detector.py:33--77}; evidence ev\_0042).

\paragraph{Distribution-focal decoding.}
The regression branch predicts, per location, a softmax distribution over
$\texttt{reg\_max}=16$ bins for each of the four box-side distances
$(\ell, t, r, b)$. The decoded distance is the expectation under that
distribution. The decoder is the \texttt{decode\_box} method
(\texttt{src/models/detector.py:79--85}; evidence ev\_0043).

\paragraph{IoU-aware classification.}
Method X gates classification logits with an IoU-aware score on the
regression branch. The flag \texttt{use\_iou\_aware} in
\texttt{configs/base.yaml} toggles the gate; the ablation in
Section~\ref{sec:experiments} reports the impact (evidence ev\_0018).
