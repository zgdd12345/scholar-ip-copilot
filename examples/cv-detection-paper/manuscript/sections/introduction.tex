\section{Introduction}
\label{sec:introduction}

Object detection has converged on two families of dense detectors: anchor-based
heads and anchor-free heads. Recent anchor-free designs reach parity with
anchor-based detectors on COCO~\citep{smith2024examplekey}, suggesting that the
per-location regression formulation is competitive. Building on this line of
work we describe Method X, an anchor-free head with the following ingredients:

\begin{itemize}
  \item A shared per-level tower~\citep{chen2023anchorfreebaseline} that
        amortises parameters across FPN levels (Section~\ref{sec:method}).
  \item A 16-bin distribution-focal regression branch with a centerness
        sibling, both implemented in \texttt{src/models/detector.py}
        (evidence ev\_0042, ev\_0043).
  \item An IoU-aware classification gate whose contribution is quantified by
        the ablation in Section~\ref{sec:experiments} (evidence ev\_0018).
\end{itemize}

The remainder of this paper describes the method (Section~\ref{sec:method}),
empirical results on COCO and VOC (Section~\ref{sec:experiments}), and the
limitations we are aware of in the current implementation
(Section~\ref{sec:conclusion}).
